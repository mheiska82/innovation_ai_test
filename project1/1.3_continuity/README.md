# Project 1.3: Continuity or break (research question 3)

*Part of [Project 1](../PROJECT1.md). Status: planned, not started.*

**Question.** Was the start-up ecosystem a continuation of the older innovation system, or a break from it?

**Why it is a separate sub-project.** Question 2 (Project 1.2) asks when the *theory of prosperity* changes. Question 3 asks whether the *institutions and instruments* behind the start-up ecosystem grew out of the innovation system (for example Tekes, Sitra, Finnvera, SHOKs, accelerators) or replaced it.

**What 1.2 already gives this question** (from [analysis v2](../1.2_paradigms/analysis_q2_v2.md), unvalidated):
- **Entrepreneurship and growth-firm finance appear from 2003 onwards**, inside the innovation-system frame (2003: C009; 2007: C119, and growth-firm risk capital with pension companies, C136).
- **2011 is the bridge:**
  - R&D funding is redirected to growth firms (C203).
  - Growth firms and risk capital are named as the bottleneck (C206).
  - Accelerators appear for the first time (C213).
- **2015 changes the instruments:** SHOK funding is wound down and Tekes grants are cut and retargeted (C274, C275).

**Plan.**
1. **Instrument coding.** Re-use the claims v0.3 file and add an instrument field: create, continue, redirect or end. Code which organisation or instrument each claim names.
2. **Continuity test.** For each start-up-era instrument, trace whether it is first named as continuing, redirecting or replacing an earlier one.
3. **Data needed:**
   - the 2008 innovation strategy (ST2008);
   - GP77 (2023);
   - the Council reviews 2010 and 2014 (Project 2 gaps);
   - Tekes/Business Finland annual reports, public versions only.
4. **Output:** a timeline of instruments with verbatim evidence, plus an analysis of continuity versus break.
