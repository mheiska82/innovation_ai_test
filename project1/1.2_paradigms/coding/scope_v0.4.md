# Coverage log for full coding (claims v0.4)

*Project 1.2. For every programme, which chapters were read in full and which were screened. Screened means: every sentence containing an economic-outcome word (growth, competitiveness, productivity, employment, exports, innovation, enterprise, competence, research, technology, cost) was read in its context. The screen alone found 25 of 41 hand-coded claims in a test, so it is used only for chapters outside the economic core.*

*v0.4 (10 November 2026) adds GP65, GP66, GP67, AN2008, GP75 and GP77. One deviation: in GP77, chapters 5 and 7, section 7.6, section 8.3 and annex D were read through a broad sentence filter instead of in full (see below and CODING_GUIDE §v0.4).*
