## 5. Observations (descriptive, no interpretation)

1. **RDI (TKI) is the organising term.** It appears about 21 times per 1,000 words in Finland's own chapters, far more than any other concept.
2. **Security and defence vocabulary is substantial.** Security, defence, resilience and dual use together come to about 89 mentions per 10,000 words in chapters 1–6. None of these is a tracker concept yet.
3. **AI is concentrated rather than spread out.** Of the 24 AI mentions in chapters 1–6, 12 are in the data chapter (4.2) and 5 in disruptive technologies (4.3). The AI rate stays high (26.6) even without the international chapter, so it is not an artefact of chapter 8.
4. **Absent vocabulary:** entrepreneurship (*yrittäjyys*), information society and industrial policy do not occur. "National innovation system" occurs only about other countries.
5. **Start-up vocabulary is marginal.** Start-ups, venture capital and growth companies together have 4 mentions in Finland's own chapters. The document approaches company growth through risk-taking and investment attraction instead (chapter 5).
6. **The structure separates *what* from *how*.** Chapter 4 says where RDI should be directed; chapter 5 says how the system should work, and the vocabulary follows that split (risk-taking in 5.1, investment in 5.2).

## 6. Interpretation (tentative; to be tested, not findings)

- **Hypothesis A (security turn).** The document may mark the arrival of security, resilience and defence as an innovation-policy rationale next to growth. This can only be tested once the earlier Council reviews (2014, 2017) and the Orpo programme have been counted with the same new concepts.
- **Hypothesis B (from system to choices).** The 1990s framing of a "national innovation system" that needs developing is replaced here by a portfolio of thematic choices plus system-level levers. This needs the 2003–2014 reviews to see when the shift happened.
- **Hypothesis C (entrepreneurship reframed).** Company growth is discussed as risk-taking and investment rather than as entrepreneurship. Whether this is a new framing or only a difference of genre needs RC2014 and RC2017.

None of these is a claim about the Council's intentions or about any named person's views.

## 7. Limitations

- **Lexical counting.** Ideas expressed in other words are missed. For example, the stem `turvallisuu` misses compounds that are not listed (such as *huoltovarmuus*).
- **A single document.** Rates from about 9,000 words rest on small counts for most concepts.
- **Genre.** It is a thematic strategy report, so frequent mentions of its own choices are expected by design and say little about salience in wider policy.
- **The provisional stems in §4 have not been validated.**
- **The appendix and reference list are excluded from the body figures.** The whole-document figures used in the earlier three-document comparison include them, which is why the two sets differ slightly. For example, RDI is 228 mentions in the body and 247 in the whole document.
